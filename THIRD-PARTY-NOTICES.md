# Third-party notices

SideRail ships three pieces of third-party material inside the extension package.
Both licences require their notice to travel with the copies they are included
in, which is what this file is for. **It must be included in any package that is
distributed** — the Chrome Web Store zip included — not just in the repository.

SideRail itself has no runtime dependencies. `playwright` is a development
dependency (Apache-2.0) used only by `test/`; it is never packaged or shipped, so
it carries no distribution obligation here.

## 3. UT1 blacklists — `vendor/exclusions.js`

The built-in list of pages SideRail never reads for page-text search: banks, webmail,
personal messaging and dating sites. Generated by `tools/build-exclusions.mjs` from four
categories (`bank`, `webmail`, `chat`, `dating`) of the UT1 blacklists maintained by the
Université Toulouse Capitole, with SideRail's own additions and a keep-list for team tools.
The generated file is data, not code, and inherits the share-alike licence of its source.

- Source: https://dsi.ut-capitole.fr/blacklists/ — mirrored at https://github.com/olbat/ut1-blacklists
- Maintained by: Fabrice Prigent, Université Toulouse Capitole
